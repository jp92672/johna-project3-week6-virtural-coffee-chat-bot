# GTM Future Sprints Document
**Author:** John Adams

Three sprints, moving from closed testing to a small real-world sample to public launch.

## Sprint 1: Closed test (POC to MVP)

1. Goal
   - Confirm the bot answers accurately, stays grounded in retrieved context, and never goes off-script before trusting it to represent me to anyone outside my own testing
2. Audience
   - People close to me (friends, peers, mentors)
3. Channel/motion
   - Direct outreach, share the chat link and ask them to try a set of real questions and provide feedback
4. Deliverable
   - 10 to 20 test conversations across a spread of question types (background, skills, behavioral)
5. Success metric
   - 80%+ confidence rated good in feedback logging, zero off-script or false claims

## Sprint 2: Hiring manager beta (MVP to Beta)

1. Goal
   - Surface real-world edge cases and breakdowns from people who actually evaluate candidates, before going fully public
2. Audience
   - HR contacts in my network, plus a few people at companies that are similar to ones I'm interested in
3. Channel/motion
   - Direct, targeted outreach, not a broad post. Personal ask to a small list
4. Deliverable
   - Feedback from a small sample of real HR/hiring-adjacent users, logged the same way as sprint 1
5. Success metric
   - No unresolved breakdowns (wrong facts, off-script replies) by end of sprint, feedback ratings stay at or above sprint 1's bar

## Sprint 3: Public launch (Beta to GA)

1. Goal
   - Get the chatbot in front of real hiring managers and recruiters to generate actual conversations and interview opportunities, not just validate the tech
2. Audience
   - Recruiters and hiring managers at companies I'm targeting, plus my broader LinkedIn network
3. Channel/motion
   - LinkedIn post announcing it, direct link added to my portfolio site, sent directly to companies I'm interested in
4. Deliverable
   - Public chatbot live on my portfolio site, LinkedIn launch post
5. Success metric
   - At least one call booked or message sent through the bot from a real hiring contact, positive feedback ratings hold steady from sprint 2
