**Author:** John Adams

## Sprint 1: Closed test (POC to MVP)

1. **Goal**
   - Confirm the bot answers accurately, stays grounded in retrieved context, resists manipulation, and is not going off-script before trusting it to represent me to anyone outside my own testing.
2. **Audience**
   - Solo build and test session myself. 
   - Extend to friends in my network to surface unknown errors and edge cases.
3. **Channel/motion**
   - Direct testing via the live chat UI and direct n8n execution testing (both manual conversations and adversarial probes: impersonation attempts, prompt injection, deliberately broken tools/retrieval).
4. **Deliverable**
   - A working, tested system: grounded RAG answers, 5 real tools (retrieval, calendar availability/booking, email), conversation logging with feedback capture, and a centralized error-alert system that catches and reports failures without exposing them to the end user.
5. **Success metric — honest status as of tonight**
   - Grounding, manipulation resistance, and tool-failure handling: tested and passing (adversarial impersonation, embedded-instruction injection, tool failures, retrieval misses — all resolved).

## Sprint 2: Hiring manager beta (MVP to Beta) — genuinely future, not started

1. **Goal**
   - Surface real-world edge cases and breakdowns from people who actually evaluate candidates, before going fully public.
2. **Audience**
   - HR contacts in my network, plus a few people at companies similar to ones I'm interested in.
3. **Channel/motion**
   - Direct, targeted outreach, not a broad post. Personal ask to a small list.
4. **Deliverable**
   - Feedback from a small sample of real HR/hiring-adjacent users, logged the same way as Sprint 1.
5. **Success metric**
   - 85% + confidence the bot is working as indented (no offscript, hallucinations, failures)
   - No unresolved breakdowns (wrong facts, off-script replies) by end of sprint, feedback ratings stay at or above Sprint 1's bar.

---

## Sprint 3: Public launch (Beta to GA) — genuinely future, not started

1. **Goal**
   - Get the chatbot in front of real hiring managers and recruiters to generate actual conversations and interview opportunities, not just validate the tech.
2. **Audience**
   - Recruiters and hiring managers at companies I'm targeting, plus my broader LinkedIn network.
3. **Channel/motion**
   - LinkedIn post announcing it, direct link added to my portfolio site, sent directly to companies I'm interested in.
4. **Deliverable**
   - Public chatbot live on my portfolio site, LinkedIn launch post.
5. **Success metric**
   - 90%-95%+ (dependent on how bad offscript/hallucinations are in previous sprints) confidence the bot is working as indented (no offscript, hallucinations, failures)
   - At least one call booked or message sent through the bot from a real hiring contact, positive feedback ratings hold steady from Sprint 2.
